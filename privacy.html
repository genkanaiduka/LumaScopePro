LUMASCOPE PRO

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 27, 2026
🔒 Zero Data Collection Policy

LumaScope Pro ("the Application") is a professional real-time video waveform, vectorscope, and color analysis application designed for colorists, post-production professionals, and video editors. Developer GEN KANAIZUKA is fully committed to protecting your privacy and confidential video assets.

1. Information and Data Collection

LumaScope Pro does NOT collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information, telemetry data, analytics, or usage statistics.

  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, or Facebook) are bundled into the application.
  • No account registration or login is ever required.
  • There are no advertisements or marketing trackers.

2. Purpose of Camera, Audio, and Screen Recording Permissions

To perform real-time video waveform and color scope calculations, the Application requests the following macOS system permissions:

  • Camera Access: Used solely to receive live video signals from Blackmagic UltraStudio / DeckLink hardware or UVC-compliant HDMI/SDI capture cards.
  • Screen Recording: Used solely to capture and crop clean feeds or program monitor windows from local NLE software (such as DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Premiere Pro, and Avid Media Composer).
  • Microphone / Audio Input: Used solely to measure real-time broadcast loudness (EBU R128 / ITU-R BS.1770-4 LUFS) and display VU/peak audio meters.

Important: All incoming video frames and audio samples are processed entirely in local GPU (Apple Silicon Metal) memory. No video or audio content is ever written to disk or transmitted over the internet, unless you explicitly choose to export a still reference snapshot to your local drive.

3. In-App Purchases and Payments

All in-app purchases and trial licensing are handled securely through Apple's native StoreKit 2 system. The developer never receives, processes, or stores your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or personal payment credentials.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be published on this website and reflected in the App Store release notes.
