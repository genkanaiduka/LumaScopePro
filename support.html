Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q. How does the 7-day free trial work?
When you first launch LumaScope Pro, you gain full, unrestricted access to all professional scopes, NLE clean feeds, and capture features for 7 days. After the trial period ends, you can unlock lifetime access for a one-time purchase of $59.99 (no monthly subscriptions or recurring fees).
Q. How do I restore my purchase on another Mac?
Click the "TRIAL" or "PRO" badge in the upper-right corner of the top menu bar, then click "Restore Purchases". As long as you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase, your license will be restored instantly at no extra charge.
Q. How do I capture live SDI / HDMI feeds from Blackmagic devices?
Connect your Blackmagic UltraStudio or DeckLink card via Thunderbolt / PCIe. In LumaScope Pro, click "MODE SELECT" in the top menu and select your device under "CAPTURE DEVICE". The app automatically locks onto incoming broadcast frame rates (from 23.98p to 60p) with zero FIFO buildup and low latency.
Q. How do I monitor DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, or Avid?
In the "MODE SELECT" dropdown, choose "DaVinci Resolve", "Adobe Premiere Pro", or "Avid Media Composer". LumaScope Pro will directly capture and analyze the program monitor feed. Use the "CROP" button to isolate a Region of Interest (ROI) such as an actor's face or color chart.
Q. What are the system requirements?
LumaScope Pro requires macOS 14.0 (Sonoma) or macOS 15.0 (Sequoia) or later. The engine is native to Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4) hardware and leverages Metal compute pipelines for zero-latency 60fps scope rendering.
